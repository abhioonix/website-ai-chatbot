# Settings Guide

## Changing your display name

Settings lets you change the name other team members see. It does not change
your login.

## Notification preferences

You can turn reminders on or off per task. A task with reminders enabled will
surface on the dashboard as its due date approaches.

## Priority levels

VaaniLink uses two priority levels: high and low. A high priority task is shown
in red, and a low priority task in yellow. There is no middle level.

## Security

Your PIN is stored as a hashed value, not as plain text. Admins can reset a
member's PIN but cannot read it.
