# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I create a task by voice

Tap the microphone button in the bottom-right corner of the screen, then speak
your task out loud. When you stop, VaaniLink shows you what it understood so you
can confirm or adjust it before saving.

## How do I reset my PIN

Open Settings, choose Security, and use the Reset PIN option. You will need your
current PIN to set a new one. If you have forgotten it, an admin can reset it for
you from the Team view.

## Can I use VaaniLink in Hindi

Yes. VaaniLink detects the language automatically, so you can speak in Hindi,
English, or another supported language without changing any setting.

## How do I add a team member

An admin can add a member from the Team view. You need the person's name, a
colour, and a four-digit PIN for them.

## How do I assign a task to someone

Open the task and pick a person from the Assign To list. Only admins can assign
a task to another person; a regular member's tasks are assigned to themselves.

## Why is my microphone button not working

The microphone needs permission from your browser. Allow microphone access for
the site, then try again. Voice recording also needs a secure connection, so use
https rather than http. If your browser does not support the Web Speech API, you
can type the task instead.
