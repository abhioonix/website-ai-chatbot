# VaaniLink Overview

VaaniLink is a multilingual voice-to-task assistant for teams. You speak in any
language, it transcribes your speech, extracts a task, and drops it onto a
shared Kanban board.

## What it can do

VaaniLink listens to you speak, works out what task you described, and creates
that task automatically. You do not have to type, and you do not have to choose
a language — it detects the language on its own.

A task can carry a name, details, a due date, a priority, and an assignee.

## Who it is for

Small teams that want to capture work by talking instead of typing. The board is
shared, so everyone sees the same tasks.

## How tasks move

A task starts as "To Do". When someone begins work it moves to "In Progress",
and when it is finished it moves to "Done". The board updates for every team
member at the same time.

## The whiteboard

The Sabha-Sadan view is a shared whiteboard. Everyone in the team sees the same
drawing, and any change appears on every screen within a moment.
